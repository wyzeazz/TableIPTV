Content Removal / DMCA Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

The Table IPTV is a self-funded, not-for-profit passion project showcasing independent music videos, most often submitted directly by artists or found and added with the intent of driving real traffic to the artist's own Bandcamp or store. If you're an artist, label, or rights holder and want something removed, this is genuinely the fastest and easiest way to make that happen — no lawyers or forms required.

If it's your content and you want it down

Email tableiptv@gmail.com with the subject line "Takedown Request: Artist - Song Title" and include:

  • The artist/track name as it appears on the channel
  • A quick confirmation you're the artist, label, or an authorized representative
  • Where you'd like it removed from (just the live channel, or also scrubbed from Recently Played / Community Top 50 history)

What happens next

Content is pulled from the active rotation within 24 hours of a verified request. No argument, no delay tactics, no hard feelings — it's your work, your call.

No monetary compensation

This channel runs at cost, self-funded, with no ads. Every "Now Playing" title links directly to the artist's own Bandcamp or store, so the actual value returned to artists is real traffic and real sales leads — not licensing fees. If that arrangement isn't right for your work, removal is always honored immediately, no questions asked.

Want to be featured instead?

If you're an artist or filmmaker who'd rather submit directly (and skip ever needing this page), check the Music Video Acquisition Form on the main site.

Questions

Email tableiptv@gmail.com — it goes directly to the one person running this, and you'll get a real reply, not an autoresponder.
