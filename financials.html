Financials & Mission Objective

Model established: August 10, 2026

The Table IPTV runs on donations, not ads. This page explains exactly where every dollar goes, in order — nothing hidden, nothing assumed. It's a real waterfall: each tier has to be genuinely filled before money moves to the next one.

*Subject to change — not because anything here is dishonest, but because this whole model is being built in real time, out loud, by one person still figuring out the exact right numbers. It'll get revised as reality provides better ones. That's the honest version of "under construction."

The Waterfall

Tier 1 — Operating Costs

VPS hosting, CDN, domain, and any tools required to keep the channel running. First claim on every dollar in, uncapped — nothing else exists if this isn't covered first.

Right now, that means a single VPS, running a single channel. That's honest, and it's enough today — but it's also a real, single point of failure. As Tier 1 grows, part of that growth is a second VPS, in a genuinely separate location, specifically so the channel can guarantee real uptime instead of hoping one machine keeps working. This isn't scope creep — it's what "operating costs" has to mean if this is ever going to become the undeniable, always-on music channel it's meant to be, not just a proof of concept.

One more honest thing worth saying plainly: if this channel ever goes genuinely viral, Tier 1 also has to cover bringing on real, paid help, fast — not eventually. A sudden, real spike in traffic or demand isn't something one person can safely absorb alone while also keeping the lights on. That's not a hypothetical being dismissed here; it's a real scenario this tier needs to be able to respond to.

Tier 2 — Founder Salary & Artist Pool

As money reaches this tier, it splits 50/50 in real time between founder salary and the artist pool — every dollar, right down the middle.

Founder's cap: a single, fixed full-time salary, adjusted annually to CPI. The exact figure isn't posted publicly — it's a real personal number, not a marketing one — but it's shared directly with committed artist-partners on request. What matters publicly is that it's capped, not open-ended.

Once founder's share for the month reaches that cap, it freezes — no further money goes to founder salary for the rest of that month, no matter how much more comes in.

Every dollar received after founder's monthly cap is reached splits differently: 30% to the artist pool, 70% forward to Tier 3, then Tier 4 (starting figures — subject to revision once real, sustained donation data exists to check them against).

This means the artist pool has no ceiling — their total share keeps growing as donations grow, unlike founder salary's fixed cap.

Artist payouts are calculated per active track (max 2-3 tracks in rotation per artist per month *subject to change) — this cap prevents any one contributor from dominating the pool through volume alone, and keeps the payout close to equal-per-artist in practice.

Worked Example

$10,000/month in Tier 2 funds, founder not yet at cap: $5,000 to founder, $5,000 to artists (straight 50/50).

$15,000/month, founder's cap reached partway through: founder still gets their capped share; the $5,000 above that point splits $1,500 to artists (30%) and $3,500 forward to Tier 3/4 (70%).

Tier 3 — Staff / Contractors

Funded from the 70% forward-flow described in Tier 2, once founder's cap is reached and there's genuinely enough coming through to sustain a role. Covers things outside the founder's scope or availability — social media management, community moderation, and similar roles, as the platform grows past what one person can sustainably run alone.

Tier 4 — Not-for-Profit Community Pot

True remainder, after every tier above is filled. Deployment is genuinely undecided (TBD) — this exists so that real growth benefits the broader community, not just the people already named in Tiers 1-3.

No Donations, No Payout

This model only ever divides money that actually exists. Nobody is owed anything from donations that were never collected — that's not a flaw in the model, it's the honest shape of a real revenue share.

What's Automated, What Isn't

Programming, scheduling, and backups run themselves — the channel keeps playing, credits keep generating, and the system heals itself without needing anyone at a keyboard.

Artist relationships, content curation, and takedown responses are handled personally — by one person, not a script. Every song on this channel was chosen, not auto-selected, and every takedown request gets a real reply within 24 hours, not an autoresponder.
