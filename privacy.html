Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

The Table IPTV is a self-funded, not-for-profit, one-person project. This policy is written the same way the rest of the site is — plainly, and honestly, so you actually know what's happening rather than reading boilerplate nobody wrote for real.

What we don't do

No ads. No ad trackers. No account sign-up required to watch. No selling, renting, or sharing any data with anyone, ever.

What actually gets stored, and where

In your browser (localStorage), on your own device: your personal Wishlist (songs you've liked), your Recently Played history, and a randomly-generated anonymous client ID used only to prevent double-voting on the Community Top 50. None of this ever leaves your device except the vote itself, sent anonymously.

On our server, when you vote/like a song: the anonymous client ID, the track, and a timestamp — stored in a database to power the Community Top 50 leaderboard. There's no name, email, or any personally identifying information attached to it, because we never ask for any.

Standard web server logs: like virtually every website on the internet, our hosting provider (OVH) and CDN/security provider (Cloudflare) automatically log basic connection data — IP address, browser type, timestamps — for security and abuse prevention. This is standard infrastructure logging, not something we actively collect or analyze about you personally.

Cookies

We don't use tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set minimal security/performance cookies as part of standard DDoS protection — these aren't used to track you across sites or build a profile.

Third-party links

Every "Now Playing" and Recently Played entry links out to Bandcamp or an artist's official store, so you can support them directly. Once you click through, you're on their site, governed by their own privacy policy — we have no control over what happens there.

Your choices

You can clear your Wishlist, Recently Played, and client ID at any time by clearing your browser's site data for tableip.tv — nothing is recoverable from our end since we never linked any of it to a real identity in the first place.

Questions

Email tableiptv@gmail.com — it goes directly to the one person running this, and you'll get a real reply.
